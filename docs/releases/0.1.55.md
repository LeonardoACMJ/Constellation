# Constellation 0.1.55 — official palettes

Starry Night now uses the approved luminous gold Stars and blue Constellations. It and Gracidea join the four existing built-in themes and are available without a personal palette library.

Matching older library entries are omitted from the selector; modified copies remain available with a custom name. Your active theme stays unchanged until you apply a palette and save. Configuration compatibility is unchanged from 0.1.54, including its optional Star label color.

Pending manual checks from the previous beta remain: intermittent audio, mixed-DPI behavior, recovery/path workflows, bundle transfer and the rare editor selection/save issue.
